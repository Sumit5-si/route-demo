## PS1 — Intelligent EV Routing & Charging Coordination

### Problem Statement

The adoption of electric vehicles is increasing, but efficient charging remains a challenge. A vehicle may need to balance travel time, battery availability, charging requirements, station availability, and changing traffic or operational conditions.

As the number of electric vehicles grows, treating routing and charging as separate decisions can lead to inefficient journeys, unnecessary waiting, and uneven utilization of charging infrastructure.

### Challenge

Build a solution that helps electric vehicles make better routing and charging decisions under changing conditions.

The solution should be able to:

* Consider the vehicle's current state and journey requirements.
* Identify suitable charging opportunities.
* Account for changing conditions during a journey.
* Balance travel efficiency with charging requirements.
* Handle situations where charging resources are limited.
* Adapt its plan when the original route or charging option becomes unsuitable.

Teams are free to determine the approach and optimization strategy best suited to the problem.

### Expected Outcome

A functional prototype capable of:

* Generating intelligent travel and charging decisions.
* Reducing unnecessary charging delays or inefficient routing.
* Adapting to changing journey conditions.
* Handling competing demand for charging resources.
* Explaining important routing or charging decisions.
* Balancing multiple objectives rather than optimizing only travel distance.

---

### Constraints

* The solution should be able to handle changing conditions rather than relying only on a static plan.
